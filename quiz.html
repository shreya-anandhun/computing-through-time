Why it looks like this

I chose a clean, modern museum style because the brief asked for an exhibit rather than an encyclopedia page, and real galleries use white space so each object gets your full attention. Black, white and one deep red keep the exhibits themselves as the colour, the way a gallery wall stays plain so the art stands out. Rounded cards and a single typeface keep it friendly for teenagers without tipping into childish.

Typeface: Google Sans Flex. Palette: black, white, red #7C0A02 (gold #FFC83D in dark mode), with two greys for surfaces and quiet text. Every drawing on the site is an original SVG.
